# Hunter Mastery — 30 Skills + 11 Passives

**Role:** The Master of Precision — a ranged killer who grows deadlier with every arrow. Low base damage, high hit count, scaling criticals. The ancestors watch every shot, learn the enemy, and guide the Hunter's aim. It's not the first shot that kills you. It's the tenth.
**Masteries per trainer:** 3 (Hunter + 2 others)
**Trainers teaching Hunter:** Grunhold, Silvergate, Deepstone, Starfall Watch

---

## Hunter Identity

**Hunter Battle Flow:**

> Range Advantage → Spirit Guidance Builds → Stack 10: Spirit Communion → Skills Transform → Every Arrow Crits → The Spirits Have Learned You

The Hunter doesn't chase. The Hunter waits — and the spirits wait with them.

- **Bow/crossbow only** — the Hunter can only equip bows and crossbows. No melee weapons. If the enemy closes to melee range, the Hunter can't swap to a sword. They must use traps and mobility to escape
- **Low base damage, high hit count** — the Hunter has the lowest per-hit damage of all masteries. But they hit multiple times per skill, and every hit builds Spirit Guidance
- **Thrown traps, not planted** — traps are thrown like grenades. Smoke bombs, flash bangs, nets, explosives. They trigger on impact, not on enemy movement. The Hunter is a battlefield controller, not a landmine layer
- **Spirit abilities** — the Hunter communes with ancestors. Spirit Walk (intangible repositioning), Spirit Copy (decoy duplicate), Spirit Bind (giant hand holds enemy). The dead fight alongside the living
- **Deadly precision, deadly critical** — the Hunter's damage comes from criticals, not raw power. The longer the fight, the more the spirits learn, the harder every arrow hits

### Spirit Guidance — Signature Mechanic

Every time the Hunter hits an enemy, **+1 Spirit Guidance stack**. Each stack = **+10% crit chance**. Stacks **never reset** — not between turns, not when the enemy heals, not when the Hunter is stunned. The ancestors are watching, and they don't forget.

**On the status bar:** `Spirit Guidance — Stack 4 (Crit +40%)`

| Stack Range | Spirit Level | Effect |
|-------------|-------------|--------|
| 1-9 | Whisper | +10% crit chance per stack |
| 10 | **Spirit Communion** | 100% crit chance. **All skills transform** — gain new spirit-enhanced effects. Ancestor spirits become visible around the Hunter. Eyes glow. Bow channels spirit energy |
| 11+ | Communion+ | Crit chance capped at 100%. **Crit damage increases +10% per stack** instead. No cap — the longer the fight, the harder every arrow hits |

**Spirit Communion (Stack 10) is the transformation.** Every skill gains a new effect — not bigger numbers, but **new behavior**. Armor ignore, uncleansable poison, unevadable arrows, persistent summons. The ancestors fundamentally change how the Hunter fights.

**The damage curve:**

| Turn (approx) | Stacks | Crit Chance | State |
|----------------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | 2-3 | 20-30% | Normal archer — weak hits, building guidance |
| 3 | 6-8 | 60-80% | Getting dangerous — crits landing more often |
| 5 | 10+ | 100% | **Spirit Communion** — skills transformed, every hit crits |
| 7 | 14+ | 100% | Crit damage +40% — arrows are devastating |
| 10+ | 20+ | 100% | Crit damage +100% — the spirits have learned everything |

The enemy's window is the first 3-5 turns. If they don't close the gap and kill the Hunter before Spirit Communion, the ancestors take over — and the outcome is decided.

### Range — Universal Ranged Mechanic

Range is a **universal combat mechanic**, not Hunter-specific. All ranged classes benefit from it.

| Class | Starting Range | Why |
|-------|---------------|-----|
| Hunter | 3 turns | Bow mastery — maximum distance, trained to fight from afar |
| Mage | 2 turns | Spell range — powerful but closer to the fight |
| Melee | 0 | No range advantage |

- During Range turns, the ranged class **always goes first**
- Each enemy turn, Range decreases by 1 (enemy closes distance)
- At Range 0, the enemy is in melee range — normal turn order resumes
- Ranged skills can **increase Range** (knockbacks, mobility, traps)
- At Range 0, ranged classes take **bonus damage from melee** (no melee weapon to defend with)

**Hunter skills that increase Range:** Knockback Shot, Backflip, Smoke Bomb, Spirit Walk, and others.

### Thrown Traps

Traps are **thrown, not planted**. They trigger on impact — like grenades, smoke bombs, and flash bangs.

**Categories:**
- **Damage traps** — Explosive Trap (fire + shrapnel), Spike Trap (thrown caltrops)
- **Control traps** — Smoke Bomb (obscures vision, +1 Range), Flash Bang (stun + blind), Net Trap (ensnared)
- **Spirit traps** — Spirit Bind (giant spirit hand holds enemy), Spirit Wall (barrier blocking movement)

Some traps synergize: Flash Bang → enemy blind → Smoke Bomb → enemy can't find Hunter → Backflip → +2 Range. The Hunter is a **distance controller** who uses traps to manage the gap.

### Spirit Abilities

The Hunter communes with ancestors from the spirit world:

- **Spirit Walk** — Hunter enters the spirit world briefly, becoming intangible. Can't be hit, can't attack. Repositioning tool
- **Spirit Copy** — Summon a spirit duplicate that distracts the enemy. Enemy may attack the copy instead of the Hunter
- **Spirit Bind** — A giant spirit hand erupts from the ground, holding the enemy in place. Ensnared + can't act
- **Spirit Bow** — Channel spirit energy into the next arrow. Arrow becomes true damage, ignores all defense

### Ambush

If the Hunter opens combat from stealth (Camouflage active), their first attack **always crits** and gains +1 Range (the enemy was caught off guard). This rewards preparation — the Hunter who plans their engagement gets a massive opening advantage.

### Turn Structure

```
Start of Turn:
  1. Range check (if Range > 0, Hunter acts first)
  2. Hunter acts (strike, trap, spirit ability, or reposition)
  3. Spirit Guidance +1 per hit landed
  4. Spirit Communion check (if stack 10 reached, skills transform for rest of combat)
  5. Enemy acts (if Range > 0, enemy spends turn closing distance)
  6. Range -1 (if Range > 0)
```

## Skill Structure

All skills use the new format — no `power` or `skill_capacity_cost`.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `power_type` | strike, defend, heal, debuff, buff, trap, spirit |
| `damage_type` | physical, magical, true (strikes/legendary only) |
| `trigger` | always, low_hp, opponent_wounded, opponent_status, opening_move, self_debuff |
| `status_apply` | Status inflicted on enemy |
| `self_status` | Status applied to self |
| `stat_mod` | Temporary stat changes — `{"self": {...}, "enemy": {...}}` |
| `mod_duration` | How many turns stat_mod lasts |
| `heal_percent` | Heals X% of max HP |
| `hits` | Number of hits per use (default 1) |
| `range_modifier` | +X Range (increases distance before enemy can melee) |
| `spirit_communion` | Effect when Spirit Communion is active (stack 10+) |

**Spirit Communion:** At stack 10, every skill transforms. The `spirit_communion` field describes the upgraded effect. Upgrades are **behavioral** — new properties, not just bigger numbers. No two skills share the same communion upgrade.

**Hunter rules:** No `damage_type: "physical"` on spirit skills (spirit = magical or true). No melee weapons. Traps are thrown (`power_type: "trap"`), not planted. Spirit abilities use `power_type: "spirit"`. All skills have a `spirit_communion` upgrade. Multi-hit skills are the Hunter's specialty. Base damage is low — crits are the damage source.
